## 1 Safety
Only a gas-qualified technician may open the casing. Isolate the electrical supply and close the gas valve before
any work inside the boiler, following the gas boiler lockout procedure. Never bypass the pressure or flame sensors.

## 3 Controls and display
The front display shows system pressure in bar and an error code when the boiler locks out. Normal operating
pressure is 1.0 to 1.5 bar when cold and up to 2.0 bar when hot.

## 6.2 Error codes
E01: ignition failure; check the gas supply and the ignition electrode.
E02: flame lost during operation; check flue and condensate drain.
E04: low system pressure, below 0.8 bar. The boiler stops to protect the heat exchanger. Repressurise to 1.2 bar
through the filling loop, then find the cause of the loss (see 7.4). Repeated E04 within weeks indicates a leak, a
failed expansion vessel or a passing pressure relief valve.
E09: overheat; the flow temperature exceeded 95 °C. Check the pump and the system valves before resetting.

## 7.4 Pressure loss
If pressure falls repeatedly, test the expansion vessel pre-charge with the system drained: it should read 1.0 bar.
A vessel below 0.5 bar has failed and must be replaced (part: 24 L expansion vessel). Check the pressure relief valve
outlet for water; a dripping outlet means the valve is passing and must be replaced. Inspect radiators and pipework
for weeping joints.

## 8 Annual service
Inspect and clean the burner, check the flue for leaks, test the safety devices and record combustion readings.
