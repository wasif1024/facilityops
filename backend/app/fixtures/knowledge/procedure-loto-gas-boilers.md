category: hvac

## Step 1 Permit
A valid gas work permit for the boiler must be on record before work starts. If it is missing or expired, stop and
raise a new permit.

## Step 2 Isolate
Switch off and lock the boiler's electrical isolator, then close and tag the gas service valve.

## Step 3 Verify
Confirm the display is off and attempt an ignition to prove the gas supply is isolated.
