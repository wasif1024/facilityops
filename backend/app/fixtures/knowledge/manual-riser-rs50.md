## 3 Couplings
Couplings can weep after pressure changes. Replace a weeping coupling with an RS-50 coupling; do not re-tighten an
old coupling more than a quarter turn.

## 4 Isolation valves
Each floor section has an isolation valve on the landing (for example V-8 for floor 8). Close the valve for the
section, open a drain point below the work, and follow the water isolation procedure.
