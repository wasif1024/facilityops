## 2 Isolation
Work inside the board requires isolation of the incoming supply under an electrical isolation permit. Prove the
board dead with an approved voltage tester before touching any conductor.

## 5 Breaker replacement
Replace a breaker with the same rating and type only (for example the 63 A DB-400 breaker). Torque terminals to the
values on the board label.

## 6 Signs of overheating
A burning smell, discoloured terminals or a breaker that is hot to the touch indicate overheating. Isolate the board
as soon as it is safe to do so and do not re-energise until it has been inspected.
