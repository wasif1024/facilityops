## 5 Overload trips
Fault P2 means the motor overload tripped. A single trip can follow a supply dip. Repeated P2 trips, especially with
noisy running, indicate worn bearings: replace the bearing kit before the motor fails.

## 6 Bearings
Fit the BP-12 bearing kit with the pump isolated and drained. Allow two hours.
