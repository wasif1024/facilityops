## 1.1 Permit
Work on any distribution board requires an electrical isolation permit and a qualified electrician. Without a valid
permit, the work must not be planned for execution.

## 1.2 Isolate and prove dead
Lock off the upstream supply, apply a personal lock and tag, and prove dead with a tested voltage indicator.
