## 4 Safety
Refrigerant work requires a certified refrigeration technician. Isolate the chiller electrically before opening any
panel. Wear eye protection when connecting gauges.

## 9.1 Compressor current
Rated running current is 42 A per compressor. A sustained draw above 48 A indicates mechanical wear or a failing
compressor; plan replacement. Short spikes during start-up up to 120 A are normal.

## 9.3 High head pressure
High head pressure is usually caused by dirty condenser coils or a failed condenser fan. Clean the coils and check fan
operation before suspecting the compressor.

## 10 Compressor replacement
Replacing a compressor needs refrigerant recovery, a vacuum test and recharge by a certified technician. Allow one
working day on site. Use only the CX90-C replacement compressor.
